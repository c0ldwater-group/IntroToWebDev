Welcome to North Star Bakery!

What We're Known For

Professionalism, Friendliness, and Great Bread!

Since our founding in 2004, North Star Bakery has been serving the Exampletown area with some of the best loaves and treats ever made. Each batch is handmade from scratch with locally-sourced ingredients, making sure every bite is perfect from start to finish.

Hours and Location

Our bakery is open 8 AM to 6 PM, Monday through Friday, and is closed on the weekends. We're also conveniently located on 123 Example St in downtown Exampletown, EX, so feel free to stop by whenever you like!
