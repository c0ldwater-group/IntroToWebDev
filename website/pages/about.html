About Us

North Star Bakery was founded in 2004 when our head baker, Chef Gusteu, decided there should be a new way to bake. After a life spent in the elite culinary world of Paris, London, and Rome, Chef Gusteu moved his life to Exampletown, where he now focuses on baking the best loaves with the best, locally-sourced ingredients. All of our baked goods use flour milled by local Exampletown farmers, with every other ingredient coming straight from their lifestock and coops.

Our employees are also all sourced from the local area, making sure North Star stays a beacon of Exampletown greatness.

Hours: 8 AM to 6 PM Monday-Friday, closed on weekends
